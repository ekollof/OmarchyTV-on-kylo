TVHACK recovery - Telefunken D65U546A / Vestel MB230

NOTE: This is the original set's tested recovery record. It names its IP
192.168.178.87, MAC 70:54:b4:fe:70:c6, local image and accepted keys. Do not
reuse that image, those keys, the IP or the MAC on another TV. Build your own
image and keys with docs/reproduce/root-payload.md.

1. Unplug the TV from mains power.
2. Insert this USB stick into the TV.
3. Hold OK on the remote while reconnecting mains power.
4. Keep OK held until the update/progress screen appears, then release it.
5. Wait about two minutes. Do not interrupt power during the update.

The active kylo_usb_update.scr restores ONLY the unsigned /conf volume from
apdat0_full.ubifs. It does not write the kernel, signed firmware or bootloader.
