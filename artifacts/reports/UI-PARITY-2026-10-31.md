# UI parity + the OSD ownership problem (2026-10-31)

> **Status: One-frame metric and BGRA fix, with historical concurrent-paint experiments. Old STBC next step withdrawn; no independent UI/startup parity.** See [report index](README.md) and
> [current corrections](../../FINDINGS.md); old plans do not authorize TV changes.

Result of the round that started with "the colors are wrong though, and it looks nothing like the
vendor aurora ui/ux". Two separate findings: a real bug in our own capture tool (fixed, and the UI
now matches the stock portal to within 1.6/255 mean pixel error), and an architectural gate that
stops us showing it on the panel while the vendor UI is running.

## 1. The colour bug was in `tools/tvshot.py`, not in the renderer

`tvshot.py` decoded every 32bpp layer as **RGBA**. The layer is **BGRA** (`byte0 = blue`), which was
already measured with `tools/colorbars` (three bands of `byte0=255`, `byte1=255`, `byte2=255`) on the
panel: left came back **blue**, middle **green**, right **red**.

Consequence: every reference capture we own had red and blue swapped, so
`artifacts/protocol/shot-launcher.png` showed a blue→purple rail with a blue Netflix logo, and that is
what the palette in `ui_fb` was sampled from. The stock portal really is:

| rail item | sampled from the swapped capture | actual colour |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `(30,148,247)` | `#f7941e` `(247,148,30)` |
| 2 | `(41,90,241)` | `#f15a29` `(241,90,41)` |
| 3 | `(45,30,190)` | `#be1e2d` `(190,30,45)` |
| 4 | `(36,25,129)` | `#811924` `(129,25,36)` |
| 5 | `(48,23,72)` | `#481730` `(72,23,48)` |

Independent confirmation: `#BE1E2D` is a literal value in the vendor style pack
(`/vendor2/dynamic_ui/telefunken18_svg.bin`, properties `title_gRowL`, `title_mRowL`, `itemPos`, …).
The corrected palette reproduces a value the firmware itself carries, which the swapped one did not.

Fixed:
* `tools/tvshot.py` — default order is now `BGRA` for 32bpp layers, with the colourbar evidence in
  the comment. `--format RGBA` still available.
* `artifacts/protocol/shot-launcher.png` — red/blue corrected in place; the pre-fix file is kept as
  `shot-launcher.as-captured.png` for provenance.
* `openaurora/modules/ui_fb/ui_fb.c` — corrected palette.

Follow-up format audit (owner clarification): raw 32bpp OSD bytes are BGRA,
whereas OAI1 assets/display blit inputs intentionally remain straight RGBA and
the display converts them. Corrected screenshot PNGs are RGB already; another
channel swap would corrupt them. RGB565 is a separate 16-bit format, not BGRA.

The audit found two additional source bugs: tvshot used Pillow's nonexistent
RGB565 image mode and applied its generic red/blue swap to that path; legacy
launcher/bootsplash/splash.c still wrote RGBA bytes to fb1. tvshot now selects an
explicit raw decoder (BGRA/RGBA or little-endian RGB565), and the legacy writer
stores BGRA with its RGB hex palette constants retained. P1/BOOTSPLASH report
claims were corrected at their source; old captures/binaries remain historical.

Offline validation: `python3 tools/tests/tvshot_decode.py` injects synthetic raw
reads through the actual CLI, forbids SSH/WoL subprocesses and checks saved RGB
PNG pixels for default/explicit BGRA, explicit RGBA and RGB565. All pass. The
legacy splash compiled with C11 -Wall -Wextra -Werror -pedantic and passed an
in-memory mixed-colour/red check on both frames without running its device path.
No TV capture/drawing, deployment or legacy/recovery binary rebuild occurred.

## 2. The layout is the portal's, not an invention

Owner clarification: full parity also includes vendor animations, transitions,
focus/selection feedback, scrolling and other UI/UX effects. This report's static
geometry and selected-frame metric do not establish any of those. Recover timing,
easing and interruption/resume behavior from firmware and validate sequences.

Measured off the corrected capture:

| element | geometry |
|---|---|
| background | `rgb(14,14,13)` |
| title | "Startpagina", ink at `x 29..290, y 80..133` (262×54) |
| rail | 5 slots, `x 24..144`, first at `y 211`, **120 px each** |
| rail, focused | grows to **150×150** at `x 11..160`, pushes the slots below down, and gets a 5 px accent frame `#f7941e` |
| rail glyphs | white line art, centred, 55–78 px |
| cards | 9 cards, `171×95`, `x 209 + i*190`, `y 478` (16:9, so logos scale exactly) |

Correction after owner observation,2026-11-01: that sampled background RGB does
not establish opacity. Our opaque clear produces a dark backdrop unlike the
transparent vendor rail. UI-TRANSPARENCY records firmware/source investigation;
the alpha/composition fix now passes actual GPU readback; independent real-video
appearance acceptance remains pending.

The rail glyphs, the app logos and the title are all the set's own content:

* glyphs cut from the device's portal framebuffer (white on coloured tile → alpha recovered by
  projecting the pixel onto `white - tile`, so the antialiasing survives);
* cards from `/buffer/downloaded_images/launcher_app_thumb<N>`, the PNGs the stock portal downloaded
  for its own `LauncherItem` rows — the same nine, in the same order, as the DB rows with
  `m_item_type=2 AND m_parent_id=1` (Netflix, Prime Video, YouTube, tabii, Canal Digitaal,
  TV Vlaanderen, TÉLÉSAT, Digiturk Play, GAIN);
* title rendered with `/vendor/fonts/DefaultProportional.ttf` (DirectFB's own default face, best
  match of the 33 fonts in that directory).

`openaurora/tools/mk-ui.py` builds all of it into the flat `OAI1` blobs `ui_fb` blits, so nothing on
the TV needs a PNG decoder.

### Measured parity

`ui_fb` painted on the set, layer read back with the fixed `tvshot`, compared to the corrected
reference:

```
whole frame   mean |Δ| 1.62/255   (0.6 %)
  background        0.67
  cards             3.22     (logo edges, 1 px)
  rail              5.71     (glyph antialiasing)
```

Per-card mean colour agrees within 1–2 counts, e.g. card 5 `(250,74,38)` vs `(250,75,39)`.

## 3. Why it cannot be *held* on the panel: both OSD layers belong to aurora

What was tried, and what each proved:

| attempt | outcome |
|---|---|
| paint fb1 once | visible, then the vendor UI repaints over it |
| mirror fb1 into both frames | stable until aurora repaints |
| add `FBIOPAN_DISPLAY` | **flicker**: our own flip made DFB flip back — hence `display.pan=0` |
| paint fb0 (`.directfbrc` says layer 0 is `middle,upper`) | the "Geen signaal" box still drew on top → **fb1 is the upper layer** |
| repaint at 4 fps / 20 fps | horizontal strips: aurora repaints **partial regions**, we write whole frames |
| `SIGSTOP` vendor aurora | **set reboots** — MCU watchdog, no kicker left |

The vendor's no-signal banner carries a live clock, so it repaints fb1 about once a second, and it
does so with dirty-rectangle updates. A second writer of whole frames can therefore never win: the
two update models interleave.

So on-panel verification of a standalone UI is blocked on **replacing aurora as the OSD owner**, not
on drawing code.

## Later corrections and remaining scope

The watchdog control path was subsequently recovered through pcbconfig/wdtctl.
Do not revive the old next step of cold NDAL STBC initialization/probing; those
calls reset the set. See PANEL-TAKEOVER for historical evidence and
[RECOVERY](../../openaurora/docs/RECOVERY.md) for the current supervisor.

The table's historical display.pan=0 choice is not a reason to skip the
FBIOPAN_DISPLAY commit. The corrected implementation disables rotation only;
every present commits, tested offline. Current OSD allocation/commit findings
are in [OSD-BUFFER-CONTRACT](OSD-BUFFER-CONTRACT-2026-10-31.md).

The pixel metric covers one selected framebuffer image. Independent cold
scanout, all-screen parity and native OmarchyTV remain unfinished.
Stock/vendor concurrent painters cannot establish exclusive ownership.
Do not stop the in-use TV for another visual comparison.

Offline navigation, 2026-11-01: mod_ui_fb subscribes to input/key. mod_ir
publishes input v1 and feeds the same 9-byte parser used for the UART read.
An empty ir.device does not open a port. Profile RC codes Up 20, Down 19,
Left 21, Right 22, Select 53 and Exit 10 move the measured rail and card row.
NEC command 0x8a is Select because the driver symbol is 0x0d. The focused
rail tile still grows to 150x150 with the 5 px #f7941e accent; a focused card
uses that accent without a separately sampled card chrome. clear() covers the
display surface (1920x1080 in the recorder). The 3-byte snapshot in that
paragraph is superseded: reload now stores band, rail, card and screen.

Offline hit, actions and animation, 2026-11-01: ui.portal hit() uses the same
rail and card rectangles as paint. The test takes centers from recorded fills.
Activating the topmost hit keeps that band and index, and the accent origin
moves up. A miss at (0, 0) does not move focus. An open screen still hit-tests
the full portal. Select opens the focused tile while animation is disabled.
Menu and Exit return to the portal and emit home and back. Aux emits source;
the index is settings get_int("m_current_source") on a one-column test database,
not a live navigation store. mod_ui_anim supplies the list truncation and the
dialog cubic progress. The dialog callback opens the tile only when done,
because setFullScreenAnimationProgress's body was not recovered. The list
callback paints the current rectangle. Rail motion stays instant. tests/ui_nav.c
passed host make check, one ASan/UBSan run (`build-owner-san`) and one ARM/qemu
run (`build-owner-arm` under the TV `/lib` namespace). This does not confirm
which live UART byte carries the RC code, and it does not open ttyS2, fbdev,
CEC or the amp. It is not cold scanout or startup acceptance.
