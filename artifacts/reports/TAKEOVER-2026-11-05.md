# Temporary openaurora takeover — 2026-11-05

Owner authorized a temporary stop of stock Aurora, with the recovery image as the safety net, and later said to kill `aurora.elf` and let the watchdog reboot. The set came back. The owner confirmed it is in control. This is not cold independent startup and not a persistent install.

Staging was `/buffer/oa-takeover` only. `/conf/oa_boot` was not created. Real `mod_plat` was not started: `plat.enable` other than `1` returns `-1` before `OA_SVC_PLAT` is published, and the enabled path runs the vendor HAL including `NDAL_VideoExt_OpenDevice(0,10)`.

## What the panel did

Watchdog disable was sent, then stock `aurora.elf` was SIGTERM'd and did not return. `openaurora` (PID 9534) owned fb1.

| check | result |
|---|---|
| Scanout | fb1 stayed on the script theme (rail 255,40,160, background 20,180,40) while Aurora was gone, then on the vendor Startpagina portal after `skin vendor` |
| Input | `ir` stayed loaded until a publish-only `plat_stub` was loaded over the control socket. `start ir` opened `/dev/ttyS2` at 115200 8N1. The demo `oa_key` returned 1 and swallowed keys until `unload lua`. `key 22` then painted band 1, and the capture shows Netflix focused |
| Fallback | `skin absent` → `requested absent active vendor state ready fallback 1 error 5`. The log is `load 'ui_absent': not found`, then `ui_vendor` reloaded and painted |
| Resume | `suspend` and `resume` both returned ok. `ir` closed and reopened the UART. `ui_vendor` painted the portal again. The capture is Startpagina with Home focused |

`display_fb` stop cleared `cleanup_pending` on the retry. `quit` left no `openaurora` process.

Captures and the host log are in `artifacts/analysis/takeover-2026-11-05/`.

## Handback

A direct `setsid` of `/vendor/com.nvt.idtv/aurora.elf` was used instead of `run_aurora.sh`, because `/conf/remote.txt` is `0:127.0.0.1:x:x` and that script takes `eth0` down. The new process did not have the appmgr environment (`Default_OSD_PLANE_Size`, `FRAMEBUFFER_2`). fb1 became 1280×1440 with pan `0,1080`, the captures were blank, and `VIDIOC_G_EXT_CTRLS`, `VIDIOC_S_INPUT` and `VIDIOC_S_STD` logged a bad file descriptor. The remote did not work.

The owner asked to kill Aurora and let the watchdog take over. `wdtctl set 10` was sent. The SoC rebooted (uptime about 80s on the next connection). After boot: `aurora.elf` PIDs 2328 and 1775, appmgr 1575, fb1 pan `0,0`, virtual `1920,2160`, no `openaurora`. `/buffer/oa-takeover` and the two restore logs were removed. `/buffer/openaurora` was left in place. `/conf/oa_boot` and `/conf/oa_fail` are absent. `/conf/oa_safe` and `/conf/coredumpnoreset` are still the Oct 31 empty mode-600 files.

## Limits

The injected Right press went through the shipped IR parser. A separate physical button trace was not captured; the UART was open, and the owner had no working remote during the failed handback. `plat_stub` is the test double in `tests/mod_plat_stub.c`. It is not the vendor bring-up. Suspend left `plat_stub` started because that double has no suspend hook.
