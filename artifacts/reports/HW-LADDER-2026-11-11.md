## Run

1. `oa-wdtfeed --node /proc/nvtstbcipc --interval 3 --timeout 10` was started
   and logged two beats before stock was touched.
2. The appmgr was paused with `SIGSTOP`; stock `run_aurora.sh`/`aurora.elf`
   were stopped. The feeder continued beating.
3. `openaurora --modules /tmp/oa-hw/modules --config /tmp/oa-hw/hw0.conf`
   started with `plat` autoloaded.
4. `plat-state` returned `plat ready 1 errors 0x0`.
5. `quit` stopped/unloaded `plat`; the process exited.
6. The feeder was stopped with its stop file; a clean `reboot` restored stock.

## What this establishes

- The real ARM `mod_plat` completed the recovered `PLAT_DRV_INI_Init` HAL
  sequence on the TV, not just against a fake HAL.
- The shipped NDAL libraries opened the platform devices and the STBC command
  socket (`iSockFd=6`); DRM mode setting completed.
- The ready receipt was written only after the autoloaded module started.

## Limits

- This is **hw0 only**. No panel power/backlight, I2S MCLK, SPI, NIM-DTV,
  source, display, UI or scanout effect is claimed.
- `plat-state errors 0x0` counts the module's GPIO/IIC/VIN error bits; it does
  not prove every HAL return or a panel/audio effect. The i2c-6/7 opens failed
  and are logged.
- The appmgr was paused and stock Aurora was stopped; this is not a cold boot
  and does not test the boot hook, watchdog ownership handoff or a persistent
  replacement.
- The owner's TV was left in stock operation. No firmware, `/conf` flag or
  persistent staging change was made.
