## Navigation path

DialogNavigator::handleNavigationMessage case 0x80 calls
doNavigateFullScreenPortal(this, true). Call site: 0xd1b7b0.

DialogNavigator::doNavigateFullScreenPortal @0xd1a890 constructs
FullScreenPortalDialog, runs it modally and logs the selected item. This is
the DialogFullScreenPortal screen (resource 114, appsGrid).

## What this establishes

The full-screen portal is reachable in shipped firmware paths; it is not
proven dead. The earlier APPS-GRID conclusion that the screen may never be
used is therefore too strong.

## What this does not establish

- No stock capture of DialogFullScreenPortal/appsGrid exists.
- The +0x1fc/+0x200 runtime inset contradiction in SVGGrid::renderBackground
  and getButtonPosition is still unresolved. The candidate 183,180,1554x720,
  5x3 310x230 geometry assumes left=right=0 and is not stock-confirmed.
- No LauncherItem row in the dumped settingsDB has m_menu_return_id 0x79, so
  the operation-0x79 UI mapping and the exact stock key path remain open.
- The screen may still be broken at runtime because of the negative inset
  values; reachability does not prove usable layout.

The prepared apps-grid manifest remains a candidate-geometry manifest pending
a corrected stock capture.
