## Resolved path

RectHandler::handleSVGElement @0xef6128 builds each element's EASIRect, then
calls the active Transform virtual +0xc before RectangleContainer::addRectangle.
For the grid cell render that virtual is
HorizontalMaskScaleTransform::transformRect @0xeecda8.

transformRect:
  - subtracts the mask origin (transformPointRelativeToMask @0xeecd0c)
  - scales x by surface_width / mask_width (getMask @0xeecc24)
  - adds the surface UI rect origin
The transformed rectangle is what RectangleContainer::getRectangle later
returns.

The HorizontalMaskScaleTransform's mask name is rowPos (the string is
0x153ae4e, referenced through the global at 0x178df30). Therefore the stored
rowPos rectangle is already mapped onto the inner surface (equal to the cell
for this portal's zero masks):
  transformed_rowPos.x  = surface.x
  transformed_rowPos.x1 = surface.x1

SVGGrid::renderBackground then computes:
  +0x1fc = (horizontal_mask_width_left + transformed_rowPos.x) - surface.x
         = horizontal_mask_width_left
  +0x200 = (horizontal_mask_width_right + surface.x1) - transformed_rowPos.x1
         = horizontal_mask_width_right

2026-11-05 review correction: the earlier right-edge subtraction was reversed.
Here surface is the inner cell rectangle after subtracting the side-mask widths,
as passed to HorizontalMaskScaleTransform::setSurfaceUIRect. The left expression
also subtracts surface.x. With zero side masks surface equals cell; with nonzero
masks the self-mask transform still cancels to the mask widths. Fresh complete
decompilation/ARM evidence: ../agent-review-2026-11-05/grid-firmware.txt.

It is not raw rowPos.x - cell.x. For DialogFullScreenPortal, the mTopL/mBotL
horizontal masks are path/polyline elements with no rectangle and the
FullScreenGridFA mRowL/mRowR rectangles are zero-width, so the portal's
resolved horizontal mask widths are zero.

The previously suspected last-cell values (-1015, +1005) are the raw
rowPos-minus-cell differences and are not the painted runtime fields.
