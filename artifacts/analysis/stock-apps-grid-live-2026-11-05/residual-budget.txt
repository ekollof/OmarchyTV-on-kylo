Focused-tile geometry oracle: stock cell_r0c0 non-orange bbox is
x 7..300, y 7..171 inside the 310x230 cell (294x165), the same as
non-focused tiles. The earlier 300x166 at +5,+7 model was wrong.

JPEG decode oracle: stage-apps-grid.py decodes JPEG thumbnails through
TurboJPEG TJFLAG_FASTDCT, the mode the shipped hal::JpegImageDecoder matches
byte-exactly (VENDOR-DECODER-DIFF-2026-11-04). Pillow's accurate-DCT output
did not match. The DAZN tile (foxxum_thumb_3753, 384x216 baseline JPEG) went
from 2.413 to 1.134 tile-only MAE, cell_r2c4 from 1.647 to 0.776, and full AE
from 2700.71 to 2457.26 (0.130% -> 0.1185%). No tile is now an outlier; the
remaining per-cell range is 0.19..0.82.

Background outside the grid/top/scrollbar is exact: bottom 0.0029,
left margin 0.0386, right margin 0.0179, gap strip 0.0128 MAE8.

Sampling colour space: a linear-light (sRGB->linear->sRGB) bilinear variant
measured worse than sRGB-space bilinear on the same corpus (0.199% vs 0.173%
AE before the focus fix), so the recorder keeps sRGB-space interpolation.
The linear-light experiment was reverted and is not committed.
